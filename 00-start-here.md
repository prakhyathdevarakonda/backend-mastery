# 00 - Start Here

## Why This Repo Exists

You want to move from:

- solving isolated interview questions

to:

- understanding backend systems end-to-end
- handling interview follow-up questions with confidence
- building practical debugging and design instincts

This repo is structured exactly for that.

## How To Study

For every topic:

1. Read the problem statement.
2. Pause and think for 5 to 10 minutes.
3. Write your own answer in a notebook.
4. Open hints.
5. Try again.
6. Open solution and compare.
7. Explain it in your own words in 2 minutes.

Do not jump directly to solutions.

## Interview Rule

For each answer, always cover:

- what failed
- why it failed
- what happens at runtime
- how to fix it safely
- tradeoffs
- how to test it
